Your local pharmacy®

Guardian, I.D.A., and Remedy'sRx pharmacies are independently owned and operated by local pharmacists who dedicate their practice to the best possible patient care — beyond just prescriptions.

With over 1,400 locations coast to coast, both in urban and rural areas, our independent pharmacies reflect the values of the communities they serve. Each pharmacy is unique, but they are all united in the pledge to uphold what being an independent pharmacy stands for — empowering patients to live a healthy life by providing unrivaled, compassionate, holistic health care.

Our history

1932

I.D.A.

The Independent Druggists' Alliance (I.D.A.) is established in Canada, providing exemplary care to patients.

1964

Guardian

The Guardian pharmacy banner is added, with the same unwavering commitment to the profession.

2006

Remedy'sRx

Remedy'sRx is founded with a commitment to building relationships with patients and offering personal attention unmatched by larger chains.

2015

Together

Remedy'sRx joins Guardian and I.D.A. — and together the three brands continue to grow as their independent owners create healthier communities from coast to coast.

Part of the McKesson Canada Retail Banner Network

While Guardian, I.D.A., and Remedy'sRx pharmacies are independently owned and operated, they are part of a family of banner brands under the McKesson Canada Retail Banner Network. McKesson Canada and its independent pharmacy owners are committed to their communities' better health, partnering with insurers, hospitals, physicians' offices, pharmacies, pharmaceutical companies and governments across the spectrum of care. To learn more, visit www.mckesson.ca.

Guardian, I.D.A. and Remedy'sRx are banners of McKesson Canada. Pharmacies operating under these banners are independently owned and operated by pharmacists.
