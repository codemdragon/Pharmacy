Compounding

Certain particular health conditions require specific medications, such as a mixture of several medicated creams, which can be prepared at the pharmacy.

Substituting a brand-name drug with a generic drug

Don't hesitate to ask your pharmacist if a generic version of a prescribed drug exists. A generic drug is pharmaceutically equivalent to a brand-name drug: it contains the same active medicinal ingredients, in the same quantities, in a comparable dosage form.
