Your pharmacist can guide you

As your partner in health, your pharmacist is not one to simply sit back on good results. They will review your current medication and treatment to further optimize your health outcomes — giving you the right tools to take your health into your own hands.

Your pharmacist can prescribe and execute certain tests to make sure your treatment is working for you, and help you keep your condition in check with monitoring tools and advice.
