Prescription Renewals

4 Ways to Renew your Medication

You can renew your medication in advance to save time and never miss a dose. Your medication will be ready at the time of your choosing and can even be delivered to your door.

Discover the many options available and tailored to you.

Contact your local pharmacy to find out if these services are available.

Where Are We

Your Renewal Options

1

The Mobile Apps

Manage your meds, anytime, anywhere: simple, fast, efficient for regular medication renewals.

From your created account, renew an existing prescription or take a picture of a new one to send to your pharmacist. You can choose the date of pick-up or delivery. The mobile app is not linked to your pharmacy record or history.

Get started today
2

Online Renewals

Ideal for long-term drug therapies and chronic medication.

With our mobile app, you can renew your medication online, set automatic renewals and even create reminders. You can view the list of all your medications and manage your family's as well. Ask your pharmacist for more information.

Access online renewals
3

Medication Synchronization

Multiple prescriptions? You can synchronize all of them at once.

This service is available for your own prescriptions and your family's. A member of the pharmacy staff will contact you once your prescriptions are due to renew and will walk you through every step. Your pharmacist takes care of everything.

See our services
4

Phone

Ideal for renewals on the spot.

You can renew your prescription directly on the phone. Dial your pharmacy's phone number and talk to a member of the pharmacy staff or leave a message on the automized voicemail. At the pharmacy, ask a pharmacy staff member to help.

Contact your pharmacy

Tip - Prescription Transfers

Switching pharmacies is easy! Simply provide us with the information of your current pharmacy and we'll do the rest!
