For short-term health conditions, we've got the solution.

Are you dealing with a minor or temporary health issue? Your local pharmacist is a valuable resource for treatment and relief. Whether it's alleviating a common cold, managing seasonal allergies, or soothing a mild skin irritation, your pharmacist is there to support your path to better health with the services below:

Prescribing for Common Conditions

Your pharmacist may be able to assess & prescribe medication for common conditions right in the pharmacy*.

Learn more *Conditions apply.

Ongoing Support & Advice

Beyond just filling prescriptions, your local pharmacist can enhance your recovery by:

  • demonstrating proper technique and best practices for using the medication
  • providing education on hygiene practices or lifestyle changes to prevent re-occurrence
  • advising to see a doctor if the condition doesn't improve with treatment
  • ensuring you feel comfortable asking questions
  • medication and allergy records for doctor appointments or travel

Prescription Transfers

If you wish to transfer your prescriptions from another pharmacy to your local Guardian, I.D.A. or Remedy'sRx, simply fill out our online form and we do the rest.

Product Recommendations

Your local pharmacist can recommend products and supplements that complement prescribed medications to alleviate symptoms of minor conditions, such as:

  • Probiotics to prevent antibiotic-associated diarrhea
  • Saline nasal spray for congestion
  • Throat lozenges for throat irritation
