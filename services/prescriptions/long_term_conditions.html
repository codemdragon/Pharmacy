Support for Chronic Conditions

For chronic conditions, we've got the solution.

Do you have a chronic condition that requires long-term medication and support? Your local pharmacist can be your partner in care throughout your treatment journey.

Contact your local pharmacy to find out if these services are available.

Where Are We

Monitoring

Your local pharmacist can play a crucial role in monitoring your medication and overall health. They can help you understand proper dosages, ensure medication adherence, and track medication effectiveness, so that you stay on the right track.

You can benefit from services that support your chronic condition, including*:

  • Chronic Disease Education
  • Health screening
  • Immunizations
  • Lifestyle advice
  • Refill reminders
  • Prescription renewals
  • Blister Packs
  • and more

Reminder Tools

With your busy lifestyle and schedule, remembering to take your medication may not rank high in your list of priorities. Fortunately, there are tools that can help you keep on track.

Pill-Packs

Using a weekly, bi-weekly or monthly pill-pack can help you organize and take your medication exactly as prescribed. Ask your pharmacist if you can benefit from this service.

Simplified Renewals

If you need to renew your medication every month, have your pharmacist set up automatic renewals, so you don't forget. Come pick your medication up every time, at the same time during the month.

Synchronization

If you're on multiple medications or manage your family's, why not pick them all at once at the pharmacy once a month? Your pharmacist can synchronize your medication renewals at the same date.

Prescription Extension

If your prescription is about to expire and you have your medical history on file, your pharmacist can extend it to save you time.*

This ensures your medication therapy is never interrupted and gives you enough time to book an appointment with your healthcare professional.

*Some fees may apply. Ask your pharmacist for details.
