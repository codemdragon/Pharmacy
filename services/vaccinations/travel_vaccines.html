Travel Vaccines

Your MarkhaMack pharmacist and the planning of your next trip.

Before you take off, get covered

If you are planning to travel, it is important to check vaccination recommendations or requirements, as these vary from country to country.

Pharmacists who offer travel vaccination services can prescribe or administer the required vaccines.

They can also check that you are up to date with your basic vaccinations and prescribe a booster shot when necessary.

Consult your pharmacist at least 6 weeks before your departure date to make sure you have the necessary vaccinations and advice.

Vaccines Available

Be sure to check whether you need any vaccines at least six weeks before your departure date, as some vaccines require more than one dose.

Cholera / Traveller's Diarrhea

Recommended if
  • You visit rural areas with limited access to potable water or where it may not be easy to apply recommended hygiene measures
  • You are at risk of complications due to any health issues you have

Japanese Encephalitis

Recommended if
  • You are planning a trip to a rural area of Asia or Australia

Yellow Fever

Recommended if
  • You are planning a trip to Africa or South America
  • You may require proof of vaccination to enter a country

Typhoid

Recommended if
  • You are planning a trip to Asia
  • You are planning a trip to Africa, the Middle East, or Latin America (especially for long-term stays), where you will visit rural areas or be in close contact with the local population

Rabies

Recommended if
  • You could be in contact with bats
  • You plan to work abroad, and your job involves contact with animals (e.g. animal conservation agent or volunteer in an animal shelter)

Hepatitis A & B

Recommended if
  • You will be visiting rural areas with limited access to potable water or where it may not be easy to apply recommended hygiene measures or health standards
  • You will engage in activities that place you at risk of infection
