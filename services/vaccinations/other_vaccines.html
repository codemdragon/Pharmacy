Vaccine-preventable diseases

Vaccination can minimize your risk of contracting several diseases, such as shingles, tetanus, hepatitis and certain childhood illnesses.

These vaccines are available for the general public. Check with your pharmacist if they are offered at our Markham location.

Vaccines available

Flu

Highly contagious infection of the respiratory tract caused by the influenza virus. Recommended every year.

Recommended for: Everyone 6 months and older.

COVID-19

Infection caused by the constantly evolving SARS-CoV-2 coronavirus. Recommended every year.

Recommended for: People aged 70 and over, or with a weakened immune system or chronic illness.

Tetanus

Caused by the bacterium Clostridium tetani, which can enter the body through a cut or bite wound.

Recommended for: Everyone. *Availability varies by province

Hepatitis A & B

Contagious liver infections caused by a virus. The chances of contracting the virus increase when traveling to certain parts of the world.

Recommended for: Everyone.

HPV

Human papillomavirus (HPV) is a sexually-transmitted virus.

Recommended for: Everyone, as of 3rd grade.

Measles

A serious and highly contagious disease, spread through the air when an infected person breathes, coughs, sneezes or speaks.

Recommended for: Children aged 12 to 18 months and all those who have never been vaccinated. *Availability varies by province

Whooping Cough

Also known as pertussis, a highly contagious disease that causes severe, uncontrollable coughing fits.

Recommended for: Infants aged 2 months and over, and pregnant women. *Availability varies by province

Pneumococcal Infections

Infections caused by the Streptococcus pneumonia bacteria.

Recommended for: People with a chronic illness or over 65.

Shingles

Shingles happen when the virus that causes chickenpox stays in your body and can reactivate at any time during your life.

Recommended for: Ages 18+ for those at increased risk, and ages 50+.

RSV

The respiratory syncytial virus (RSV) is a seasonal virus that circulates from fall to spring.

Recommended for: People aged 60 and over.
